# BA Draft Quality Checklist

Use this checklist for quick self-review after generating a BA draft.

## Structure

- The draft follows the correct template
- Main headings are present
- Empty sections are either omitted or clearly marked

## Content

- Business intent is visible
- Actors or stakeholders are identified when relevant
- Scope is understandable
- Requirements or needs are written clearly

## Consistency

- Terminology is consistent
- Repeated concepts use the same wording
- Contradictions are called out

## Uncertainty

- Assumptions are labeled
- Open questions are explicit
- Source gaps are listed where needed

## Restraint

- No invented workflows
- No invented rules
- No unnecessary technical solutioning
- No fake metrics or timelines

## Readiness

- A BA can review and refine it
- A stakeholder can understand what is still missing
- The next conversation with the client is easier because of this draft
